# Veterinary CT/MRI Report Skills

AI-assisted (Claude) report-writing skills for canine and feline CT and MRI studies.

[![Register for testing](https://img.shields.io/badge/Register-for%20testing-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge)](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdfyfjVhCn9ndbWfejvUbu3pcD5fRVK4Xeg9_HMdyM_NryHZQ/viewform)

🇹🇷 Türkçe: [veteriner-bt-mr-rapor-skill](https://github.com/201621/veteriner-bt-mr-rapor-skill)

## What it does

- Turns the clinician's raw findings or dictation into a structured, standardized CT/MRI report.
- Follows a systematic region-by-region reading order with standard normal statements.
- Writes the Conclusion as finding → interpretation → recommendation.
- Runs a pre-delivery checklist to catch missing regions and measurements.

Distilled from roughly 160 CT and 160 MRI reports by an experienced veterinary radiologist. Reports are currently produced in Turkish.

## Scope

- **CT skill:** head, thorax, abdomen, spine and whole-body studies
- **MRI skill:** brain, cervical and thoracolumbar spine, stifle and neck studies

## How to join the test

1. Fill in the [registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdfyfjVhCn9ndbWfejvUbu3pcD5fRVK4Xeg9_HMdyM_NryHZQ/viewform) (in Turkish).
2. After your application is reviewed, the skill files and setup steps are sent to your email.
3. Upload the skills to your Claude account (paid plan), try them on anonymized cases and share your feedback.

## Important

- Generated reports are drafts; clinical decisions and sign-off remain with the reviewing veterinarian.
- Do not use owner-identifying information in test cases.
- Skill files are not published in this repository; they are shared only with registered testers.
